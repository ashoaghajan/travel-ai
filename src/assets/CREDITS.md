# Image credits

Mock imagery used by the Stage 1 UI. All files were resized/cropped locally.

> **Note on the activities explorer.** Screen 6 also shows photographs fetched
> at runtime from Wikimedia Commons, chosen by the Wikidata entity id that
> OpenTripMap reports for a place. Those are not listed here — they vary by
> destination and cannot be enumerated ahead of time. Their author and licence
> come back with the image and are rendered on the card itself, linking to the
> Commons file page; see `src/services/wikimedia.service.ts`. Places with no
> photograph fall back to the category artwork below, which is credited here.
